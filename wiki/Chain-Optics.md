# Optics chain: focus lenses

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** four focus lenses: Quartz, Ember, Glacite and Void.
**What you get:** the lenses decide what the [Laser Etcher](Components.md#laser-etcher-and-lenses) can etch,
and what a [Cutting Lance](Tools.md#cutting-lance-and-focus-lenses) cut does. A lens is never used up.

All four grow in the [Crystal Grower](Components.md#crystal-grower) in water.

| Lens | Offworld | Standalone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quartz | four xertz quartz and a glass pane | four nether quartz and a glass pane |
| Ember | four cindrite and a glass pane | four blaze powder, two quartz and a glass pane, slowly |
| Glacite | four glacite and a glass pane | four Synthetic Glacite and a glass pane |
| Void | two voidglass (Nyxara) or crystal shards and a glass pane | two Void Crystal and a glass pane, slowly |

| Lens | In the Laser Etcher | In the Cutting Lance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quartz | Logic Chip | half the cooldown |
| Ember | fast Logic Chip from cindersteel wire | smelted drops |
| Glacite | Quantum Chip from two wafers | ice and glass come away whole |
| Void | Quantum Chip | twice the reach |

## Machines

Crystal Grower, Laser Etcher.
