# Tech Guide

The **Tech Guide** is NeroTech's in-game handbook — a guided tour from your first Machine Frame to
a running fusion plant, added in 0.1.0-beta.1. It comes in two forms:

- The **Tech Guide pedestal** — a projector block. Place it in your base and it holographically
  projects **your next incomplete step** above itself, spinning and bobbing, so your current goal
  is literally visible from across the room.
- The **datapad** — a hand-held version of the same guide for on the go.

Both open the same screen: chapters on the left, steps with icons and descriptions on the right,
with completed steps ticked.

## Progress is advancement-driven

The guide keeps no progress of its own — each step completes when you earn its **advancement**, so
guide progress is exactly your advancement progress (per player, and it works retroactively: steps
you have already done are already ticked).

## The chapters

Eight chapters, twenty-two steps, mirroring NeroTech's arc:

1. **First Power** — craft a Machine Frame, build a Nero Generator and a Solar Array, give the
   array clear skies.
2. **Processing** — Ore Processor and Fabricator, then their Advanced tiers.
3. **Consequences** — manage [heat](Thermal-System.md); meet your first Dirty Filter.
4. **Mitigation** — build a [Scrubber and a Remediator](Pollution-and-Mitigation.md).
5. **Automation** — Auto Crafter, Item Sorter, and the
   [Configurator](Side-Config-and-Configurator.md).
6. **Analytics** — place an [Analytics Terminal](Analytics.md).
7. **Fusion** — cast Fusion Casing, form a [reactor shell](Fusion-Reactor.md), burn a Stellar
   Cell.
8. **Exotic Matter** — widen the [accelerator ring](Particle-Collider.md) until its stretches run
   eleven blocks or more, collide two Stellar Cells into antimatter, build a
   [Singularity Vault](Exotic-Endgame.md).
