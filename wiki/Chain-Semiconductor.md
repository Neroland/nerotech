# Semiconductor chain: wafers and processors

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** Crystal Boules, Wafers, the **Logic Chip** and the **Quantum Chip**.
**What you get:** the component ladder above the Circuit Board, which every tier-2 and tier-3 machine needs.

## Offworld route

1. A Crystal Seed (an amethyst shard and a quartz, crafted, two out).
2. The seed with two xertz quartz and one brine salt (Greenxertz, Europa), or with four xertz quartz, grown in
   water in the [Crystal Grower](Components.md#crystal-grower): a Crystal Boule.
3. The boule in the Plate Press with the Wafer Die: four Wafers.
4. Wafers etched in the [Laser Etcher](Components.md#laser-etcher-and-lenses): Logic Chips, then Quantum
   Processors.

## Standalone route

1. The same seed.
2. The seed with four nether quartz grown in water: a boule, more slowly.
3. The same press and etching steps.

## Etching

| Product | Inputs | Lens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logic Chip | a Wafer and two copper wire | Quartz |
| Logic Chip (fast) | a Wafer and two cindersteel wire | Ember |
| Quantum Chip | a Wafer, a Logic Chip and two Superconductor Wire | Void |
| Quantum Chip | two Wafers and two Superconductor Wire | Glacite |

The lens stays in the etcher. See the [Optics chain](Chain-Optics.md) for how lenses are made.

## Machines

Crystal Grower, Plate Press, Laser Etcher.
