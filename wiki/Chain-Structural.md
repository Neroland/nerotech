# Structural chain: Cindersteel and titanium

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** Cindersteel ingots, plates and rods, and titanium plate.
**What you get:** tier-2 machine hulls, the Blast Foundry, drill heads, tool bodies, the Fuel Injector.

## Offworld route

1. Cindrite (Cindara) with iron dust in the [Plasma Crucible](Metallurgy.md#plasma-crucible), or cindrite with
   iron dust or raw hematite (Mars) in a heated [Blast Foundry](Metallurgy.md#blast-foundry) for bulk.
2. Ingots into plates and rods in the Plate Press (Plate Die, Rod Die); ingots into Cindersteel Wire in the
   Filament Spooler.
3. Titanium: a titanium ingot from Luna's ilmenite in the Plate Press with the Plate Die. Titanium plate makes
   two Heat Sinks where cindersteel makes one.

## Standalone route

1. Two iron dust, one coal and one blaze powder in the Plasma Crucible, or the same in a heated Blast Foundry.
2. The same press and spooler steps as offworld.

Every standalone ingot takes a blaze powder, so a standalone server wants a blaze farm. Titanium has no
standalone route on purpose: everything that takes titanium plate also takes cindersteel plate.

## Side product: acid ore leaching

With [sulfuric acid](Chain-Chemistry.md), the Reaction Vat turns one raw iron, copper or gold ore (or raw
hematite) into four dusts.

## Machines

Plasma Crucible, Blast Foundry (Foundry Brick, Foundry Heat Port), Plate Press, Filament Spooler.
