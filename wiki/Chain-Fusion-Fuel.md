# Fusion fuel chain: deuterium, helium-3 and europium

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** deuterium and helium-3 gases, Europium Dust and the Europium Flux Lattice.
**What you get:** a [gas-fed fusion tier](Fusion-Fuel.md) with no cells to craft, and a calmer reactor.

## Offworld route

1. Deuterium shards or cells in the Cryo Distiller: four units of deuterium.
2. Helium-3 canisters in the Cryo Distiller: two units of helium-3.
3. Raw europium from Europa's vent fields in the Centrifuge: Europium Dust (sometimes gold dust too).
4. Europium Flux Lattice: crafted (one), or in the [Component Forge](Components.md#component-forge) from fusion
   casing, europium, cindersteel, a Quantum Chip, superconductor wire and cryo coolant (four).

## Standalone route

1. Deuterium: Electrolyzer hydrogen and a little water in the Centrifuge, one unit per unit of hydrogen.
2. Helium-3: three Centrifuges side by side, facing the same way, form a
   [cascade](Chemistry-and-Cryogenics.md#centrifuge-and-cascades). Three deuterium into the first give two,
   the second gives two, the third gives one helium-3. This is the slowest standalone product in the mod.
3. Europium Dust: eight glowstone dust in the Centrifuge, slowly.
4. The same lattice recipes.

## Using it

Pipe deuterium and helium-3 into Fuel Injectors in a 7x7x7 Fusion Reactor shell (two parts deuterium to one
part helium-3), and replace up to eight casing blocks with Europium Flux Lattices. See
[Fusion Fuel](Fusion-Fuel.md).

## Machines

Cryo Distiller, Centrifuge, Electrolyzer, Component Forge, Fuel Injector, Europium Flux Lattice.
