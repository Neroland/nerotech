# Chemistry chain: acid, resin and gases

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** sulfuric acid, polymer resin and Polymer Sheet, carbon dioxide and methane.
**What you get:** acid ore leaching (four dusts from one ore), Capacitors, Polymer Sheet for the Scrubber
Module and Capacitors, Gas Turbine fuel, cryo coolant and calcite.

## Offworld route

- **Sulfur:** Cindara sulfur carries `c:dusts/sulfur` already; raw vent sulfide in the
  [Centrifuge](Chemistry-and-Cryogenics.md#centrifuge-and-cascades) gives two sulfur.
- **Sulfuric acid:** sulfur, water and oxygen (from the Electrolyzer) in the
  [Reaction Vat](Chemistry-and-Cryogenics.md#reaction-vat).
- **Polymer resin:** amber sap (Greenxertz) and methane in the Reaction Vat; pressed into Polymer Sheet with
  the Plate Die.
- **Methane:** methane crystal or methane ice in the Cryo Distiller. **Carbon dioxide:** dry ice in the Cryo
  Distiller, or the Atmospheric Condenser on Mars or Cindara.

## Standalone route

- **Sulfur:** two gunpowder in the Centrifuge.
- **Polymer resin:** two logs and methane in the Reaction Vat.
- **Methane:** coal and hydrogen in the Cryo Distiller. **Carbon dioxide:** coal and oxygen in the Cryo
  Distiller, or the Atmospheric Condenser in the Nether.
- **Nitrogen:** the Atmospheric Condenser in the Overworld, slowly.

## Uses

| Material | Used in |
| --- | --- |
| Sulfuric acid | acid ore leaching, Capacitors |
| Polymer Sheet | Capacitors, Scrubber Module |
| Methane | polymer resin, Gas Turbine fuel (twice hydrogen's yield per unit) |
| Carbon dioxide | cryo coolant, calcite |
| Nitrogen | cryo coolant, packed ice, Heat Exchanger coolant |

## Machines

Centrifuge, Cryo Distiller, Atmospheric Condenser, Reaction Vat, Process Tank, Plate Press.
