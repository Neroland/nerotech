# Superconductor chain: glacite wire and coils

Part of NeroTech's six offworld production chains. Every chain has two routes: **offworld**, with Nerospace resources taken by tag, and **standalone**, on a server with only Neroland Core and NeroTech, at roughly three to five times the energy or time. A recipe viewer shows the exact numbers.

**Product:** Superconductor Wire and the Superconductor Coil (tier 3).
**What you get:** tier-3 coils for the Fuel Injector, the Cutting Lance, the Glacite Drill Head, the
Europium Flux Lattice and the Quantum Chip.

## Offworld route

1. Glacite from Glacira.
2. Cryo coolant from packed cryo ice or dry ice (with a little nitrogen), or frost shards, in the
   [Cryo Distiller](Chemistry-and-Cryogenics.md#cryo-distiller).
3. Two glacite and two Cindersteel Wire in the Filament Spooler make two Superconductor Wire.
4. Four Superconductor Wire round a tier-2 coil, with a Heat Sink and 250 mB of cryo coolant, in the
   [Component Forge](Components.md#component-forge) make the Superconductor Coil.

## Standalone route

1. **Synthetic Glacite:** one Void Crystal, four snow blocks and two packed ice in 100 mB of cryo coolant in
   the [Crystal Grower](Components.md#crystal-grower), four out. It carries the same tag as glacite, so every
   glacite recipe takes it. Faster in the cold.
2. Cryo coolant from four snow blocks and water with nitrogen (from the Atmospheric Condenser) or carbon
   dioxide in the Reaction Vat. Packed ice comes from snow and nitrogen in the Cryo Distiller, so no Silk Touch
   is needed.
3. The same spooler and forge steps as offworld.

Without Nerospace, Void Crystal comes from the [Particle Accelerator](Particle-Collider.md), so echo shards are
the real limit.

## Machines

Cryo Distiller, Atmospheric Condenser, Reaction Vat, Crystal Grower, Filament Spooler, Component Forge.
